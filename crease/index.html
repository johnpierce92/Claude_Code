    How to play

    A country is hidden somewhere on the map. Each clue folds away the countries it rules out.

    1. Read the clue and type a guess, or tap a country on the map.
    2. A wrong guess, or Next clue, reveals another clue.
    3. You get up to 8 clues. The fewer you need, the better.

    Positions are measured from each country's center. A new country is hidden every day at midnight UTC. Practice rounds don't count toward your stats.
