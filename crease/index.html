How to play

A country is hidden on the map. Narrow it down by folding the map until you can name it.

  1. Ask yes/no questions. Each answer folds every ruled-out country away. Questions with a dashed border fold the paper along a line of latitude or longitude.
  2. Guess by typing a name or tapping a country. A wrong guess folds that country away.
  3. You have 10 creases. Each question and each wrong guess uses one. A correct guess is free.

Answers use each country's geographic center. There's a new puzzle every day at midnight UTC. Practice rounds don't affect your stats.
